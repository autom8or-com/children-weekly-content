## Opening (after the 12-scene story)

> *What is Angela documenting today? **Mighty Hand, Tender Care.*** God has a mighty hand against every Pharaoh, but He also has a tender care for every child who obeys Him. The Passover shows both at the same time — judgment and rescue, side by side. The blood on the door is what makes the difference: those who obey are passed over; those who ignore it are not. And the Passover lamb is a picture of Jesus, the Lamb of God, whose blood covers us forever.

## Point 1 — Pray for those who despitefully use you

- **Pull picture:** `scene-3/3a` (Israelites ask for silver and gold; the Egyptians give freely)
- **Scripture:** Exodus 12:35–36; Matthew 5:44
- **Teacher's Note:** The Israelites had been enslaved and mistreated for generations. God could have told them to take revenge, but instead He told them to ASK the Egyptians for their wages (Exo 12:35-36) — and the Egyptians gave freely. Blessing, not cursing, is the way of the Kingdom (Matt 5:44).
- **Angela's diary line:** *"I should pray for those who despitefully use me. I certainly will because the children of Israel got the wages for their labor before departure."*

## Point 2 — The blood of the Lamb must be on me

- **Pull picture:** `scene-1/1c` (the marked door — single brush of red on lintel, family safe inside)
- **Scripture:** Exodus 12:13; 1 Corinthians 5:7
- **Teacher's Note:** The destroyer passed over every house marked with blood. The Passover lamb is a picture of Jesus — the Lamb of God whose blood covers us (1 Cor 5:7; 1 Pet 1:18-19). Without the blood, there is no covering.
- **Angela's diary line:** *"The blood of Jesus must be on me as a sign that I am a child of God, else the messenger of death will kill me as he killed the Egyptians."*

## Point 3 — Whatever God promises, He fulfills

- **Pull picture:** `scene-2/2c` (Pharaoh at midnight, summoning Moses and Aaron — "Rise up! Go!")
- **Scripture:** Exodus 12:31–33, 51
- **Teacher's Note:** God told Moses He would deliver Israel with a mighty hand (Exo 3:19-20; 6:1). On that very night, Pharaoh rose in the dark and begged Israel to leave. God's word never returns empty (Isa 55:11).
- **Angela's diary line:** *"God promised Moses that he will deliver the Israelites by a powerful hand and he did. Whatever God promises me, He will fulfill. I believe!"*

## Point 4 — Keep hearing God, no matter what

- **Pull picture:** `scene-1/1a` (God tells Moses the calendar; Moses and Aaron listening, the cloud and God-figure in the sky)
- **Scripture:** Exodus 12:1, 21; Mark 9:23
- **Teacher's Note:** Moses heard God and kept believing even after Pharaoh refused him time and again. His relationship with the Lord was never broken. *"All things are possible to him that believes"* (Mark 9:23) — a child who keeps hearing God can face any Pharaoh.
- **Angela's diary line:** *"I like to hear God like Moses heard and believed despite Pharaoh's past disobedience."*

## The Bridge (New Testament echo)

- **Pull picture:** `scene-4/4a` (Jesus with the Passover lamb at his feet — the Lamb of God)
- **Scripture:** John 1:29; 1 Corinthians 5:7
- **Teacher's Note:** The Passover lamb was a one-night sacrifice that saved one family. Jesus is the once-for-all Lamb who saves the whole world. When you see the blood of Jesus applied to your heart, the destroyer has no claim on you. This is the heart of the gospel for kids: there is a covering, and it is not your own goodness.
