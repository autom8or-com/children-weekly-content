## Opening (after the 12-scene story)

> *What is Angela documenting today? **Stiff-necked Pharaoh.*** Every time God sent a warning, Pharaoh stiffened his neck instead of bending his heart. God is patient, but His patience is a warning, not a promise that tomorrow will always come. **Now** is the day of salvation.

## Point 1 — God is never late

- **Pull picture:** `scene-1/1a` (Moses warns Pharaoh at dawn)
- **Scripture:** Exodus 9:18
- **Teacher's Note:** God set the hour in advance — "about this time tomorrow" (Exo 9:18). He is never early and never late; His timing always plays out in His children's favour.
- **Angela's diary line:** *"God is never late. Things will play out in my favour."*

## Point 2 — Call on the name of the Lord and be saved

- **Pull picture:** `scene-1/1b` (those who sheltered vs those who left their animals out)
- **Scripture:** Exodus 9:19–21
- **Teacher's Note:** Those who feared the Lord's word brought their servants and flocks inside and were saved from the hail (Exo 9:20–21). Calling on the Lord — heeding His word in trouble — is what rescues.
- **Angela's diary line:** *"Whoever calls the name of the Lord will be saved. Those who took their flocks inside were saved. If I ever call the name of the Lord in trouble, I will be saved."*

## Point 3 — Be sincere when you say sorry

- **Pull picture:** `scene-1/1e` (Pharaoh's fake sorry)
- **Scripture:** Exodus 9:27–35
- **Teacher's Note:** Pharaoh said "I have sinned… the Lord is right" (9:27), but the moment the hail stopped he hardened his heart again (9:34–35). Calling yourself a sinner is not the same as being sorry.
- **Angela's diary line:** *"Not everyone who calls himself a sinner is really sorry. Pharaoh called himself a sinner but was never sorry — soon he hardened his heart. I must be sincere when I ask the Lord for forgiveness."*

## Point 4 — Do not ignore godly counsel

- **Pull picture:** `scene-2/2b` (officials pleading with Pharaoh)
- **Scripture:** Exodus 10:7
- **Teacher's Note:** Pharaoh's own officials warned him — "do you not yet realize that Egypt is ruined?" (10:7) — and he would not listen. A child who will perish ignores godly counsel and instruction.
- **Angela's diary line:** *"A child that will perish will ignore godly counsel and instruction. Pharaoh's officials warned him, but he would not listen."*

## Point 5 — God wants your TOTAL freedom

- **Pull picture:** `scene-2/2d` (Pharaoh's partial offer; Moses driven out)
- **Scripture:** Exodus 10:8–11
- **Teacher's Note:** Satan, like stubborn Pharaoh, does not like the total freedom of a child of God. Pharaoh offered a partial deal — "go, but only the men" (10:8–11). God's freedom is never partial.
- **Angela's diary line:** *"Satan, like stubborn Pharaoh, does not like the total freedom of a child of God. He said to Moses, 'Go with only the men to worship God.'"*

## Point 6 — The one God speaks to is powerful

- **Pull picture:** `scene-2/2a` (Moses declaring the locust warning)
- **Scripture:** Exodus 10:5–6
- **Teacher's Note:** Moses could declare "not a hut will remain" untouched by locusts because he spoke for God. A child who walks with God can face a bully — and report it — trusting God to deliver.
- **Angela's diary line:** *"One whom the Lord speaks to is very powerful. Moses said to Pharaoh, 'Not a hut will remain.' When you are bullied in class, you can face the bully and report it, trusting God to deliver you."*

## Point 7 — Light in a dark world

- **Pull picture:** `scene-3/3b` (Goshen bright while Egypt sits in darkness)
- **Scripture:** Exodus 10:21–23
- **Teacher's Note:** Thick darkness covered Egypt for three days, yet the Israelites had light where they lived (10:22–23). We live IN this dark world as light, but we are not OF it.
- **Angela's diary line:** *"Goshen had light while the rest of Egypt was in darkness. I live in this world as light, but I am not of this world that is full of darkness."*

## Point 8 — God speaks to me as I need Him

- **Pull picture:** `scene-3/3a` (Moses hears from God and stretches out his hand)
- **Scripture:** (cross-cutting — the Exodus 9–10 pattern)
- **Teacher's Note:** Scripture never records Moses climbing a mountain in this whole stretch; God spoke to him per need, mouth-to-mouth. His relationship with the Lord was never broken, and neither is ours.
- **Angela's diary line:** *"God spoke to Moses mouth-to-mouth. He can speak to me too. His relationship with the Lord was never broken."*
