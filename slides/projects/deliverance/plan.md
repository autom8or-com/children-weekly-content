# Deliverance — Slide Deck Plan
Topic: Deliverance (Pre-K / Toddler Sunday School)
Audience: Pre-K children
Art Style: Storybook Illustration (flat digital, clean outlines, warm vibrant colors)
Slide Ratio: 16:9 (widescreen)

---

## Characters
| ID       | Name    | Description |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| angela   | Angela  | Chocolate brown-skinned African toddler girl, ~3yo, round chubby face, wide expressive dark eyes, short natural hair in two small puffs, bright pink pinafore dress over white long-sleeved shirt, white sneakers |
| jesus    | Jesus   | Superhero in bright blue Superman-style suit, flowing red cape, bold golden "J" on red chest shield, warm golden-brown skin, kind eyes, short dark hair, strong heroic build |
| moses    | Moses   | Youthful Nigerian man, chocolate brown skin, athletic build, short natural hair, bright EMERALD GREEN Superman-style costume, golden "M" on breastplate, holding a long wooden rod |
| aaron    | Aaron   | Youthful Nigerian man, chocolate brown skin, similar build to Moses, bright SUNSHINE YELLOW Superman-style costume, golden "A" on breastplate, holding a long wooden rod |
| serpent  | Serpent | Gigantic cartoon snake — exaggeratedly enormous, emerald green with darker scales, large cartoonish yellow eyes with sinister grin, wide open mouth with comical fangs |

## Scenes

### Scene 1 — What is Deliverance?
- **1a** · Angela chased by serpent (text-to-image, refs: angela + serpent)
- **1b** · Jesus rescues Angela, snake flees (edit from 1a OR text-to-image, refs: angela + jesus + serpent)

### Scene 2 — Heroes of Our Deliverance
- **2a** · God sends Moses and Aaron — divine commission (text-to-image, refs: moses + aaron)
- **2b** · Moses & Aaron before Pharaoh, Serpent in background (text-to-image, refs: moses + aaron + serpent)
- **2c** · Israelites oppressed — sympathetic scene (text-to-image, no main character refs)

### Scene 3 — Who is the Victim?
- **3a** · Angela + crowd pursued by multiple serpents (edit, refs: angela + serpent)
- **3b** · Garden of Eden — serpent in tree (text-to-image, refs: serpent)
- **3c** · Jesus flies in with Bible over the crowd (edit from 3a, refs: jesus)
- **3d** · Serpents gone, people rejoice (edit from 3c — scene continuation)

### Scene 4 — Why Does Jesus Set Us Free?
- **4a** · Angela serves water to Jesus in sitting room (edit, refs: angela + jesus)
- **4b** · Angela & Jesus read Bible in garden (edit, refs: angela + jesus)
- **4c** · Angela wears her "A" cape (edit from 4b — scene continuation)
