# Flies, Livestock and Boils — Plan

**Passage:** Exodus 8:20 – 9:12 (NIV) — the fourth, fifth and sixth plagues.
**Sequel to:** `frogs-and-gnats` (plagues 2 & 3). Same cast, same storybook style.
**Story theme (the deck):** God's escalating judgment on Egypt, and the *distinction* he draws to protect his people in Goshen.
**Weekly lesson theme (Angela's corner):** **Incomplete / partial obedience** — Angela documents **"Sneaky Pharaoh."**
**Audience / tone:** Pre-K storybook, gentle. Flies = dense dark buzzing swarms. Dead livestock = peaceful lying-down, no gore. Boils = simple gentle red polka-dot spots, never graphic.

## Two layers

1. **Story progression (18 scenes, this pipeline).** Tells plagues 4–6 scene by scene.
2. **Angela's corner (manual, by Odun).** The teaching layer assembled afterward by pulling selected pictures from the story scenes and pairing them with diary lines. See `lesson-notes.md` for the point → scene-picture → Teacher's Note → Angela line map.

The story scenes are deliberately staged so the four "Sneaky Pharaoh" moments each exist as a clean, pull-able frame (marked 📌 below).

## Memory Bubble Format (God speaking to Moses)

Same device as `frogs-and-gnats`: God's instruction is drawn **inside an AI-generated cloud-shaped memory/thought bubble** above Moses — connected by small thought-bubble circles, with `god-a` rendered inside and a small speech bubble carrying God's words. No PIL pip. Produced entirely by the edit prompt, passing `god-a` as a ref for divine-figure consistency. Chain per pair: **God spoke → Moses heard (memory bubble) → Moses relayed → action follows.**

## Continuity Strategy

- **Don't strip the base; disturb it as little as possible.** Memory-bubble openers (1a, 2a, 3a) keep the full rttb `scene-2/2a` cast (Pharaoh + officials + Moses + Aaron) and only add the God bubble + Moses's relay speech.
- **Never reposition Moses/Aaron in a cross-edit** — moving a figure forces a redraw, which drifts appearance. Pin them in place and re-anchor youth via `moses-b`/`aaron-a` refs.
- Palace scenes anchor to `gods-power scene-2/2a` (Pharaoh + magicians) or `scene-2/2b` (Pharaoh facing Moses & Aaron), and `scene-2/2c` (magicians) — the cleanest direct ref applications.
- New settings with no prior base (Goshen homes, dead livestock field, boils street) are `text-to-image`, described inline. This also sidesteps the safety-filter risk of pairing refs with distress prompts.

## CTAs (light — Angela's corner carries the real teaching)

- **1/1f:** "Pharaoh said yes, then said no. Obeying God halfway is still saying no."
- **2/2f:** "He saw God spare Israel and still held back. Partial obedience has painful rewards."
- **3/3f:** "Keep refusing God, and the heart grows harder. Obey fully. Obey today."
