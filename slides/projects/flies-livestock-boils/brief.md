## 5. Story beats (ordered → illustrations + story slides)
### Plague 4 — Flies (Exo 8:20-32)
- God sends Moses to warn Pharaoh by the river
- Swarms of flies fill an Egyptian home / the land
- Goshen stays clear and bright — God's distinction
- Pharaoh half-agrees, flies leave, then he breaks his promise
### Plague 5 — Livestock (Exo 9:1-7)
- Moses warns Pharaoh: tomorrow the animals
- Egyptian livestock fall sick in the field (gentle)
- Israel's animals stay healthy in Goshen — distinction
### Plague 6 — Boils (Exo 9:8-12)
- Moses tosses soot/ashes toward the sky
- Soft red boils break out on people and animals
- Pharaoh stays hard-hearted, magicians can't stand
