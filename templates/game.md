# GAME SUNDAY TEMPLATE
## 3 Mechanic-Based Rounds × 5 Narrative Sections
### (Adaptable to ANY Bible Passage)

---

## OVERVIEW
- **Time:** 55 minutes total
- **Participants:** 20-30 teens in 5 teams of 4-6
- **Structure:** 3 rounds organized by game mechanic, each incorporating all 5 narrative sections
- **Tone:** Conservative biblical answers guided toward clear truth

---

## HOW TO USE THIS TEMPLATE

### Step 1: Identify Your 5 Narrative Sections
Break your Bible passage/chapter into **5 key narrative sections** based on major events, characters, or turning points:

**Template:**
- **Section 1:** [Opening event / Key character introduction / Initial problem]
- **Section 2:** [Development / Conflict / Action taken]
- **Section 3:** [Crisis / Opposition / Challenge]
- **Section 4:** [Turning point / Repentance / Transformation]
- **Section 5:** [Resolution / Conclusion / Broader principle]

**Example (Acts 19):**
- Section 1: Baptism & The Holy Spirit (19:1-7)
- Section 2: Paul's Teaching & Miracles (19:8-12)
- Section 3: The Sons of Sceva (19:13-16)
- Section 4: The Great Burning (19:17-20)
- Section 5: The Riot (19:21-41)

### Step 2: Create Key Biblical Principles
For EACH section, identify:
- **What Happens:** Brief summary of events
- **Key Principle:** The spiritual truth/lesson from that section

**Template for Each Section:**
```
SECTION [#]: [Title]
What Happens: [2-3 sentence summary of events]
Key Principle: [The spiritual truth teens should understand]
```

### Step 3: Prepare the 3 Rounds

---

## THE 3 GAME ROUNDS

### ROUND 1: FIND & QUOTE ROUND (15 minutes)

**Game Mechanic:** Teams hunt for 5 key phrases (one from each section) in the Bible passage and quote the verse reference correctly.

**How It Works:**
1. Distribute printed copies of the Bible passage to each team
2. Call out one phrase at a time (see template below)
3. First team to find it and quote the reference correctly gets points
4. Move through all 5 phrases sequentially

**Preparation Steps:**
1. Read through your passage and identify ONE key phrase from each section
2. The phrase should be:
   - **Distinctive enough to find** (not too common)
   - **Directly quoted from Scripture** (word-for-word or close)
   - **Representative of that section's event**

**Phrase Template:**

| Section | Phrase to Find | Expected Reference |
|---------|---|---|
| 1 | "[KEY QUOTE FROM SECTION 1]" | [Book Chapter:Verses] |
| 2 | "[KEY QUOTE FROM SECTION 2]" | [Book Chapter:Verses] |
| 3 | "[KEY QUOTE FROM SECTION 3]" | [Book Chapter:Verses] |
| 4 | "[KEY QUOTE FROM SECTION 4]" | [Book Chapter:Verses] |
| 5 | "[KEY QUOTE FROM SECTION 5]" | [Book Chapter:Verses] |

**Scoring:**
- Each phrase found correctly: 5 points
- First team to find all 5 phrases: +10 bonus points
- Correct reference quoted: +2 points per phrase

**Debrief After Round 1:**
"You hunted through the passage and found the key moments. This is how Scripture reading works—we search, we discover, and we find God's truth."

---

### ROUND 2: COMPREHENSION ROUND (15 minutes)

**Game Mechanic:** You ask 5 comprehension questions (one per section). Teams/pairs answer with depth. Quality of answers = points.

**How It Works:**
1. Ask one question from each section (in order)
2. Allow 2-3 minutes per question for discussion/thinking
3. Teams discuss among themselves, then one representative answers
4. Judge answers based on biblical accuracy and depth of understanding
5. Award points for correct, thoughtful answers

**Preparation Steps:**
1. For EACH section, write ONE deeper-thinking comprehension question
2. The question should ask:
   - **WHY** something happened (cause/effect)
   - **What it reveals** about God or faith
   - **The significance** of the event
3. Write a **Biblical Answer Guide** for each question (what the correct answer should include)

**Question Template:**

| Section | Question | Biblical Answer Guide |
|---------|----------|----------------------|
| 1 | [DEEP QUESTION about Section 1] | [Expected answer should include...] |
| 2 | [DEEP QUESTION about Section 2] | [Expected answer should include...] |
| 3 | [DEEP QUESTION about Section 3] | [Expected answer should include...] |
| 4 | [DEEP QUESTION about Section 4] | [Expected answer should include...] |
| 5 | [DEEP QUESTION about Section 5] | [Expected answer should include...] |

**Scoring:**
- Correct, biblically grounded answer: 10 points
- Partial credit for partial understanding: 5 points
- Show of biblical reasoning and thoughtfulness: +3 bonus points

**Debrief After Round 2:**
"These questions weren't about trivia—they were about understanding WHY these things happened and what they mean. That's how we grow in biblical knowledge."

---

### ROUND 3: BINARY JUMP ROUND (15 minutes)

**Game Mechanic:** Everyone assembles in the middle. You call 5 true/false statements (one per section). Teams jump to RIGHT (true) or LEFT (false). Correct jumps = points.

**How It Works:**
1. Everyone assembles in the center
2. Call out a statement clearly
3. Give 3 seconds for teams to jump to RIGHT or LEFT
4. Announce correct answer and explain briefly
5. All teams that jumped correctly get points
6. Move to next statement

**Preparation Steps:**
1. For EACH section, write ONE true/false statement
2. The statement should:
   - Test understanding of a key truth from that section
   - Be clearly true OR clearly false (no ambiguity)
   - Include a brief explanation for why the answer is correct

**Binary Jump Statement Template:**

| Section | Statement | Correct Answer | Explanation |
|---------|-----------|---|---|
| 1 | "[TRUE/FALSE STATEMENT about Section 1]" | TRUE or FALSE | [Why this is correct] |
| 2 | "[TRUE/FALSE STATEMENT about Section 2]" | TRUE or FALSE | [Why this is correct] |
| 3 | "[TRUE/FALSE STATEMENT about Section 3]" | TRUE or FALSE | [Why this is correct] |
| 4 | "[TRUE/FALSE STATEMENT about Section 4]" | TRUE or FALSE | [Why this is correct] |
| 5 | "[TRUE/FALSE STATEMENT about Section 5]" | TRUE or FALSE | [Why this is correct] |

**Scoring:**
- Each correct jump: 5 points per team that jumped correctly
- All participants engage: Universal participation reinforces learning

**Debrief After Round 3:**
"Notice what you just learned: [Connect to overarching biblical truth of the passage]"

## OVERALL GAME TIMING (55 minutes)

| Activity | Duration | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Setup & Explanation | 5 min | Divide teams, explain rules, distribute Bibles |
| Round 1: Find & Quote | 15 min | Hunt for 5 phrases from 5 sections |
| Round 2: Comprehension | 15 min | Answer 5 deep questions |
| Round 3: Binary Jump | 15 min | Jump RIGHT/LEFT for 5 statements |
| Tally Scores & Announce Winner | 3 min | Celebrate winning team |
| Final Discussion & Reflection | 2 min | Connect game back to biblical truth |

**Total: 55 minutes**

[PERSONALIZE based on the 5 sections—how do they connect to teens' lives?]

## KEY ELEMENTS TO ESTABLISH FOR EACH PASSAGE

Before you fill in the template, determine:

1. **What are the 5 natural narrative sections?**
2. **What is the overarching biblical principle** that ties all 5 together?
3. **For each section:**
   - What key quote/phrase captures it?
   - What comprehension question tests true understanding?
   - What true/false statement tests discernment?
4. **How does this passage speak to teens' lives today?**

## NOTES FOR FACILITATOR

✓ Have Bible copies ready (printed or digital)  
✓ Pre-read the entire passage and identify your 5 sections  
✓ Prepare all questions and answers BEFORE the session  
✓ Ensure all teams participate equally in all 3 rounds  
✓ Use Biblical Answer Guides to correct misunderstandings gently  
✓ Celebrate effort and growth, not just winning  
✓ Connect each round back to the core biblical truth  
✓ Make it about **learning Scripture deeply**, not just competition
