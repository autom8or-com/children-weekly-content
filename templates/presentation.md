# PRESENTATION SUNDAY TEMPLATE
## 5 Topics × 4 Presentation Format Options
### (Adaptable to ANY Bible Passage)

---

## OVERVIEW
- **Preparation Time:** 1 week advance notice
- **Participants:** 20-30 teens in 4-5 strategic groups
- **Group Assignment:** Known 1 week ahead (time to prepare)
- **Structure:** Groups choose their own presentation format
- **Timing:** 55 minutes total on Sunday
- **Evaluation:** Observed for engagement/understanding (not graded)
- **Distribution:** ONE guideline document with all 5 topics sent to groups

---

## HOW TO USE THIS TEMPLATE

### Step 1: Identify Your 5 Narrative Sections
Break your Bible passage into **5 key narrative sections**:
- Section 1: [Opening event / Introduction]
- Section 2: [Development / Conflict]
- Section 3: [Crisis / Opposition]
- Section 4: [Turning point / Transformation]
- Section 5: [Resolution / Broader principle]

### Step 2: Create Topic for Each Section
For each section, write:
- **Topic Title** (brief, engaging)
- **Section Summary** (2-3 sentences of what happens)
- **Key Questions for Groups to Explore** (3-4 questions they should answer through their presentation)
- **Biblical Principle** (the spiritual truth of that section)

### Step 3: Explain the 4 Format Options
Groups choose ONE of these presentation approaches:

**Format 1: STORYTELLING**
- Retell the section in their own words
- Make it engaging and clear
- Help the audience understand what happened
- Connect events to the biblical principle

**Format 2: CHARACTER ANALYSIS**
- Focus on the main character(s) in the section
- Analyze WHY they made their choices
- Explore their motivations, fears, faith (or lack thereof)
- Connect character decisions to the biblical principle

**Format 3: PRINCIPLE & APPLICATION**
- Extract the biblical principle from the section
- Explain what God is teaching through this event
- Give a modern teen scenario that shows this principle in action
- Help audience see how this applies to their lives

**Format 4: Q&A STYLE**
- Prepare 2-3 thought-provoking questions about the section
- Ask the class to discuss/answer
- Guide discussion toward the biblical principle
- Summarize the key truth at the end

### Step 4: Set Expectations
Each group should know:
- **Time limit:** 2-3 minutes per group (adjust based on 4 or 5 groups)
- **All members participate:** Everyone should speak/be involved
- **Reference the Scripture:** Quote or reference the passage
- **Show preparation:** It should be clear they read and thought about the section
- **Aim for clarity:** Help the audience understand the section and its meaning

### Step 5: Prepare Discussion Connector
After all presentations, you'll lead a 10-minute discussion connecting all 5 sections. Prepare:
- 2-3 discussion questions that tie sections together
- Key biblical truth that runs through the whole passage
- How this truth applies to teens today

## PRESENTATION SUNDAY TIMING (55 minutes)

| Activity | Duration | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Welcome & Passage Recap | 5 min | Briefly remind everyone of the passage & its context |
| Group 1 Presentation | 2-3 min | Topic from Section 1 |
| Group 2 Presentation | 2-3 min | Topic from Section 2 |
| Group 3 Presentation | 2-3 min | Topic from Section 3 |
| Group 4 Presentation | 2-3 min | Topic from Section 4 |
| Group 5 Presentation | 2-3 min | Topic from Section 5 (or split between groups) |
| *Subtotal Presentations* | *35 min* | *(For 5 groups × 7 min each; adjust if 4 groups)* |
| Discussion: Connecting All Sections | 10 min | Facilitate discussion about overarching truth |
| Closing Remarks & Reflection | 5 min | Summarize biblical principle and application |

**Total: 55 minutes**

---

## DISCUSSION CONNECTOR (10 minutes)

**Purpose:** Tie all 5 sections together and show how they teach one overarching biblical truth.

**Preparation (before Sunday):**
Write 2-3 discussion questions that connect the sections. Examples:

**Sample Discussion Questions:**
- "How do all 5 sections of this passage show us [OVERARCHING PRINCIPLE]?"
- "What do you notice about how God worked in Section 1, Section 2, Section 3, Section 4, and Section 5? How is His character revealed throughout?"
- "If you had to summarize this entire passage in one sentence, what would it be?"
- "How does this passage change how you think about [TOPIC]?"

**During Discussion (10 min):**
1. Ask first question, let 2-3 teens respond
2. Ask second question, let 2-3 teens respond
3. Summarize connections they're making
4. Transition to closing remarks

---

## CLOSING REMARKS (5 minutes)

**Template for Your Closing Statement:**

"Today we heard 5 presentations about [PASSAGE NAME]. Each group shared a different part of the story, but together, they teach us one powerful truth: **[STATE OVERARCHING BIBLICAL PRINCIPLE]**.

In Section 1, we saw [brief reference to Section 1].  
In Section 2, we saw [brief reference to Section 2].  
In Section 3, we saw [brief reference to Section 3].  
In Section 4, we saw [brief reference to Section 4].  
In Section 5, we saw [brief reference to Section 5].  

All of these together show us that **[PRINCIPLE]**.

This means for YOU, as a teenager, [HOW THIS APPLIES TO THEIR LIVES].

As you leave today, I want you to remember [CALL TO ACTION/KEY TAKEAWAY]."

## STRATEGIC GROUP ASSIGNMENT

**How to Assign Groups (1 week ahead):**

**Goal:** Mix ages, abilities, and confidence levels. Pair strong presenters with quieter teens.

**Strategy:**
- Group 1: Strong leader + 1-2 quieter teens + 1 confident speaker
- Group 2: Confident speaker + mixed abilities
- Group 3: Mixed abilities + one reflective thinker
- Group 4: Strong leader + newer/younger teens
- Group 5 (if applicable): Mixed ages + one natural communicator

**Avoid:**
- All quiet teens in one group
- All older/younger teens in one group
- All confident speakers in one group (they don't need the challenge as much)

## SAMPLE TOPIC (Using Acts 19 as Example)

```
TOPIC 1: BAPTISM & THE HOLY SPIRIT (Acts 19:1-7)

WHAT HAPPENS IN THIS SECTION:
Paul finds disciples of John the Baptist in Ephesus who haven't received the Holy Spirit. Paul baptizes them in Jesus' name and lays hands on them. They receive the Holy Spirit and speak in tongues and prophesy.

KEY QUESTIONS YOUR GROUP SHOULD EXPLORE:
1. What was different about John's baptism vs. Jesus' baptism?
2. Why did Paul baptize these disciples a second time?
3. What does receiving the Holy Spirit mean in this passage?
4. How does the Holy Spirit work in a teenager's life today?

THE BIBLICAL PRINCIPLE:
True faith in Jesus brings the gift of the Holy Spirit. Baptism is not just a ritual—it's the beginning of a transformed life in God's power.

FORMAT OPTIONS:
- STORYTELLING: Tell the story of these disciples and their baptism experience
- CHARACTER ANALYSIS: Analyze why these disciples were willing to be baptized again; explore their journey from John's baptism to Jesus'
- PRINCIPLE & APPLICATION: Teach about the Holy Spirit and show how a teen today might experience God's power in their life
- Q&A STYLE: Ask the class what they understand about the Holy Spirit and guide them to discover the principle

PRESENTATION TIME LIMIT: 2-3 minutes
```

## NOTES FOR FACILITATOR

✓ Groups choose their own format—this gives ownership and flexibility  
✓ 1-week notice allows proper preparation without rushing  
✓ All group members participate—no one person dominates  
✓ Focus on engagement/understanding, not perfection  
✓ Discussion connector ties everything together into one overarching truth  
✓ Closing remarks cement the biblical principle  
✓ Strategic group assignment ensures balanced participation  
✓ This format builds confidence in quieter teens (they have a week to prepare)  
✓ Varied formats keep the hour interesting (not 5 identical presentations)  

---

## KEY PRINCIPLE

Presentation Sunday is about **peer teaching and community learning**. When teens prepare and teach each other, they learn more deeply. The variety of presentation formats ensures that different learning styles are engaged and that preparation doesn't feel like a burden—groups can choose how they're most comfortable sharing.
